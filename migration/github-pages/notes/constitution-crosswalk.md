# Top-down constitution crosswalk

**Status:** Non-normative drafting map. This is a sequence for our protocol, not a claim that UIL or TAPPS has adopted it.

| UIL constitutional area | Standard work | Current location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name, objectives, scope | State the Standard's name and purpose for interscholastic competition by schools serving home-educated students. | `standard/00-purpose-and-conformance.md` |
| Definitions | Define school, student, team, contest, conformance track, and later terms as their rules are developed. | `standard/00-purpose-and-conformance.md`; `standard/20-student-eligibility.md` |
| Eligibility for Signatory Status | Set school qualifications before an adoption agreement can be accepted. | Initial concept in `standard/05-schools-and-tracks.md` |
| Attainment of Signatory Status | Require execution and acceptance of an adoption agreement. | Initial concept in `standard/05-schools-and-tracks.md`; full procedure pending |
| Signatory Dues | Specify any dues through a published schedule and adoption agreement, including due dates and nonpayment consequences. | Heading and reservation in `standard/05-schools-and-tracks.md` |
| Standing | Address renewal, continuing obligations, suspension, termination, reinstatement, and the effect of standing on representation and contest entry. | Initial concept in `standard/05-schools-and-tracks.md`; full rules pending |
| Mandatory Suspension | Specify any non-discretionary suspension grounds and the corresponding decision, notice, review, and restoration procedures. | Reserved heading in `standard/05-schools-and-tracks.md` |
| Organization and administration | Identify participating schools, each chief administrator, the steward, a contracted program administrator, authorized actions, and later decision makers. | Discussion draft in `standard/06-organization-and-administration.md`; governance options in `notes/governance-options.md` |
| Classification of Violations; Protests | Classify violations, define who may protest, and establish reporting, investigation, decision, and review procedures. | Reserved in `standard/07-classification-of-violations-and-protests.md` |
| Committee Meetings and Appeals | Develop meeting, hearing, and appeal rules after the relevant decision bodies are defined. | Reserved headings in `standard/08-committee-meetings-and-appeals.md` |
| Standard and Amendments | Develop notice, hearings, adoption, official interpretations, construction, and amendment procedures. | Reserved headings in `standard/09-standard-and-amendments.md` |
| Classification, Modules, and Grouping | Reserve module selection, possible school classifications or groupings, and review; do not assume UIL-style conferences or districts. | Reserved in `standard/09a-classification-and-grouping.md` |
| Non-Discrimination in Contests | Develop a general policy and activity-specific applications under applicable law. | Reserved in `standard/09b-non-discrimination-in-contests.md` |
| Contests | Develop contests and pilots, an official calendar, rescheduling, and any non-traditional-school-year rule. | Reserved headings in `standard/09c-contests.md` |
| School classification and conformance | Common core plus UIL, TAPPS, or core-only track; later define track-aligned seasons and contest rules. | `standard/05-schools-and-tracks.md` |
| Student eligibility | HS-BASE, immutable competition-grade history with a limited pre-high-school repeat, age, non-graduation, and four-year window. | `standard/10-hs-base.md`; `standard/20-student-eligibility.md` |
| Waiver of Eligibility Rules | Define waiver applications, decisions, review, retroactivity, and false information. | Reserved headings in `standard/21-waiver-of-eligibility-rules.md` |
| Awards | Address limits on student awards and gifts or awards to sponsors and coaches. | Reserved headings in `standard/22-awards.md` |
| Records, Publications, and Finances | Reserve records, publication, and finance provisions for detailed later work. | Chapter-level reservation in `standard/23-records-publications-and-finances.md` |
| Activity rules and calendars | Define seasons and contest-specific restrictions, allowing later UIL- or TAPPS-aligned schedules. | Pending |
| Enforcement and records | Certification, challenges, proof, penalties, appeals, and record retention. | Pending |

The proposed school category is framed so UIL or TAPPS could adapt it through their own rulemaking. Under current rules it is a Standard category only. The three conformance tracks are declarations under this Standard, not memberships in external organizations. This map follows the broad order of UIL's subjects without adopting UIL's enumeration or all of its definitions.
