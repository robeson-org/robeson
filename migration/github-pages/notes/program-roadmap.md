# Program roadmap and governance questions

**Status:** Non-normative planning notes. These objectives do not claim that a governing authority or partner organization has agreed to administer the standard.

## Intended reach

- Offer a common interscholastic standard or protocol that home-school programs can adopt.
- Let Newton Prep follow the eventual standard as one adopter among others.
- Give UIL, TAPPS, and SPC schools confidence in the eligibility, conduct, and accountability of participating home-school programs.
- Explore whether a Home School Member category within UIL, or possibly TAPPS, would make sense after the protocol is developed and tested.

## Governance to develop

- Select a steward for the protocol. Options include an independent nonprofit corporation, a public benefit corporation, or a host organization operating under a defined agreement. A steward could contract with a qualified organization for program administration.
- Establish a process for payments to officials, complaints about treatment of officials, investigation, decisions, remedies, and appeals. This is intended in part to address TASO's need for a responsible authority.
- Define adoption, verification, enforcement, recordkeeping, and relationships with existing interscholastic organizations.

These subjects belong in future standard modules and supporting implementation materials. HS-BASE v0.5 addresses only the home-education classification.

See [governance and publication options](governance-options.md) for a working comparison. No entity form, administrator, or public license has been selected.
