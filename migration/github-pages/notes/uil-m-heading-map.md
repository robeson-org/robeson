# UIL Subchapter M: eligibility heading map

**Status:** Non-normative drafting map. The [UIL 2026–27 eligibility provisions](https://www.uiltexas.org/policy/constitution/general/eligibility) provide a subject sequence; they do not automatically govern signatory schools.

| UIL subject | Proposed Standard heading or treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Student's Eligibility for All UIL Contests | Student's Eligibility for All Contests; incorporate HS-BASE and the cumulative eligibility requirements. |
| Eligibility — Academics | Keep heading; develop activity-specific provisions. |
| Eligibility — Music | Keep heading; consider whether broader performing-arts provisions are also needed. |
| Eligibility — Athletics | Keep heading; develop sport-specific provisions. |
| State Law | Keep heading; identify applicable law without making every UIL rule part of the common Standard. |
| High School Graduate | Keep heading; use the current non-graduation draft as the starting text. |
| Full-Time Day Student | Replace with **Home-Education Status**. Signatory schools roster students but need not provide their education; HS-BASE supplies the relevant relationship test. |
| Regular Attendance | Reserve or replace after defining what continuing participation with a signatory school requires. Do not import classroom-attendance language. |
| Four-Year Program of High School Courses | Keep heading; adapt the draft's competition-grade declaration and four-year clock. |
| Recruiting | Keep heading; develop rules for school affiliation and inducements. |
| Eligibility Burden of Proof | Keep heading; develop evidence and review rules. |
| Credit Requirements for Eligibility During First Six Weeks | Place UIL-specific assessment and parental-verification provisions in the UIL academic-conformance track; decide whether the common Standard needs a separate academic-standing rule. |
| Accommodations for Disability | Keep heading; develop accessible procedures and exception criteria. |
| Amateur Athletic Status | Keep heading for athletics. |
| Residence in School District and Attendance Zone | Replace with **Residence**; any geographic rule remains open. |
| Changing Schools for Athletic Purposes | Keep heading provisionally; clarify whether it addresses changes among signatory schools, other schools, or both. |
| Age | Keep heading; the existing September 1 proposal is the starting text. |

The existing [eligibility discussion draft](../standard/20-student-eligibility.md) contains substantive work on grade declaration, age, graduation, and the four-year window. Its sections can be reordered into this sequence as the headings and corresponding rules are settled. This map makes no change to those proposed rules.
