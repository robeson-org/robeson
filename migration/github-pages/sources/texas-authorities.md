# Texas authorities and citation notes

**Status:** Non-normative research notes. Verify the current law and program terms when applying or revising the standard.

## Core home-school authorities

- **Texas Education Code § 29.916(a)(1).** Defines “home-schooled student” for that section using predominant instruction in a general elementary or secondary program provided by a parent or person standing in parental authority in or through the child's home. [Texas Legislature, Chapter 29](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/docs/ED/htm/ED.29.htm).
- **Texas Education Code § 33.0832.** Governs participation in UIL activities by non-enrolled students and refers to instruction described in § 29.916(a)(1). Its academic verification provisions belong in a later UIL academic-conformance option. [Texas Legislature, Chapter 33](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/ED/pdf/ED.33.pdf).
- ***Texas Education Agency v. Leeper*, 893 S.W.2d 432, 443–44 (Tex. 1994).** Foundational Texas Supreme Court decision on bona fide home schools within the compulsory-attendance private-school exemption. [Texas Education Agency home-school guidance](https://tea.texas.gov/families-and-students/finding-school-your-child/home-schooling); [Texas Supreme Court's later discussion of *Leeper*](https://www.txcourts.gov/media/1400120/140732.pdf).

These authorities provide the legal backdrop. HS-BASE's four reserved parental authorities, canonical academic record, and bright-line exclusions are **additional proposed standard requirements**, not claims that those exact tests appear in the cited authorities.

## TEFA category distinction

Texas Education Freedom Accounts distinguish the award for a child attending a participating private school from the smaller home-school/other category. The amount can change, so HS-BASE refers to the **private-school funding category** rather than a fixed dollar figure. The proposed exclusion depends on the school's category and the student's enrollment in its general program, even when that student receives no award. [Texas Comptroller TEFA program overview](https://educationfreedom.texas.gov/); [TEFA school participation information](https://educationfreedom.texas.gov/schools/).

## Later academic option

Section 33.0832's provisions for a nationally recognized norm-referenced assessment and subsequent parental academic verification should be considered when drafting the separate UIL academic-conformance option. HS-BASE does not use statewide testing or parental academic assessments as an exclusion.
