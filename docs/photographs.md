The football photograph is an alternative with an explicit rights record to the supplied Paul Robeson House image-only URL. The supplied Flickr item is Charles Alston's 1943 biographical illustration, not a photograph; it remains a possible future addition. The Emperor Jones frame and other PICRYL images were reviewed as alternatives but are not used in this layout.

Biographical sources: [Rutgers](https://robeson100.rutgers.edu/about-paul-robeson), [National Park Service](https://www.nps.gov/places/new-york-paul-robeson-home.htm), and [National Archives](https://www.archives.gov/education/lessons/robeson). The site's educational connection is Newton Prep's interpretation, not a claim of endorsement by Robeson or his estate.

Additional biographical sources: [Rutgers on Robeson’s college achievements](https://www.rutgers.edu/news/paul-robeson-all-around-excellence) and [football career](https://www.rutgers.edu/news/paul-robeson-football-star). These corroborate details brought to our attention by [Corey Quinlan Taylor’s essay](https://coreyquinlantaylor.wordpress.com/2022/02/13/pruner-paul-the-greatest-college-football-story-youve-never-heard-of/).
